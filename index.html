World Models for Robot Learning

Motus Robotics is the official research hub for the Motus family of embodied-AI and world-action-model projects. Explore the papers, models, code, and real-robot demonstrations.

Motus

A unified latent action world model that integrates understanding, action, and video generation. CVPR 2026.

Read the Motus paper on arXiv

Motus2

A self-evolving General World Model for dexterous manipulation with closed-loop policy improvement, working memory, and tactile sensing.
