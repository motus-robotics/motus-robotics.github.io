# Motus Robotics

Official research website for **Motus** and **Motus2**, a family of world-model and
world-action-model projects for robot learning and embodied AI.

## Featured research

### Motus2

**Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation** unifies a
policy, simulator, and evaluator in one shared video-action model. It combines
egocentric human-data scaling, value-guided closed-loop policy improvement, working
memory, and tactile sensing for real-robot dexterous manipulation.

- [Official Motus2 project page](https://motus-robotics.github.io/motus2/)
- [Motus2 中文介绍](https://motus-robotics.github.io/motus2/zh/)
- [arXiv:2608.30237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)
- [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)

### Motus

**Motus: A Unified Latent Action World Model** integrates understanding, action,
and video-generation experts through a unified latent-action formulation. The work
was presented at CVPR 2026.

- [Official Motus project page](https://motus-robotics.github.io/motus)
- [arXiv:2512.13030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13030)
- [CVPR 2026 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Bi_Motus_A_Unified_Latent_Action_World_Model_CVPR_2026_paper.html)
- [Code](https://github.com/thu-ml/Motus)
- [Models](https://huggingface.co/motus-robotics)

## Related research

[MotuBrain](https://www.motubrain.cn/en/) is a separate world-action-model project
for robot control developed by overlapping collaborators. Motus, MotuBrain, and
Motus2 have distinct papers and official resources.
